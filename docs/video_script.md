# Demo video script (2:45, Telegram)

Record a real run. Anything on a fictional or test portal must be labelled on screen.
Never show a real Aadhaar, phone, bank number, OTP or password.

| Time | Screen | Voice-over |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | A government portal, scrolling through rules | "This student qualifies for a scholarship. Finding it, and getting the form right, is the hard part." |
| 0:15–0:35 | Telegram: Tamil voice note "Scholarship apply pannanum"; consent button; 4 quick questions as buttons | "She asks in Tamil, on Telegram. Four quick questions." |
| 0:35–0:55 | Ranked schemes, each with its quoted rule and source link; she taps one | "Every scheme comes with the exact rule and where it's from. No guessing." |
| 0:55–1:15 | Document photos sent; profile fields with source tags; doctor flags "S Satyajit" vs "Satyajit S" with Confirm / Upload again | "It reads her certificates, and catches a name mismatch before the portal rejects it." |
| 1:15–1:45 | Web app: browser logs in (OTP typed in Telegram), fills the form live; CAPTCHA arrives on Telegram, she replies | "It fills the portal itself. CAPTCHA and OTP go to her; it never bypasses them." |
| 1:45–2:10 | Judge presses the chaos button: a label renames; the agent fails, reasons, finds the field, continues | "Portals change. Here we break the page on purpose. It notices, repairs, and carries on." |
| 2:10–2:30 | Review screenshot on Telegram; one edit; Approve; receipt as a Tamil voice note | "Nothing is submitted until she approves. The receipt comes back in Tamil." |
| 2:30–2:45 | Second run in replay mode with a timer; benchmark chart | "The second time, it replays what it learned, and the timer shows how long that took. Others explain schemes. We apply for them." |
